About Faranani Shoe Cleaning

Faranani began as a small local service that grew into a community-first business: providing professional shoe care while training and empowering young people. We blend technical excellence with social impact.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to provide high-quality shoe restoration and maintenance while offering skills training and employment opportunities to youth in our community. We envision a future where local craft and dignity of work go hand-in-hand with environmental responsibility.
